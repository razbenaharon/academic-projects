# Publication Hygiene

This repository is an allowlisted public copy of academic work. Originals remain outside Git.
Do not copy entire course folders or private audit reports into this repository.

Before staging new work, inspect filenames, source, notebook cells, outputs and metadata,
documents, embedded media and generated exports. An ignore rule does not remove a tracked
file and is not a substitute for content review. Keep credentials in environment variables;
do not embed them in config files, examples, notebooks or generated HTML.

Imported notebooks are distributed without outputs, widget metadata or embedded submission
images. Curated historical public venue/fixture CSVs are explicitly allowlisted; other
datasets and generated model/index artifacts are ignored by default.

Identifier/credential cleanup also covers reachable branch history. History cleanup does
not revoke a credential, and hosting-service caches, old pull-request refs and third-party
copies may retain previous objects. Exposed credentials must be rotated by their owner.
